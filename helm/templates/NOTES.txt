curfew is running in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Opt a Deployment or StatefulSet in with annotations:

  curfew/sleep: "0 20 * * 1-5"     # cron: scale to 0
  curfew/wake: "0 8 * * 1-5"       # cron: restore replicas
  curfew/timezone: "Europe/Paris"  # optional, default UTC

Or annotate a namespace to schedule all its workloads, optionally excluding some:

  curfew/exclude-kinds: "StatefulSet"
  curfew/exclude-labels: "tier=data,keep-alive"

Sleeping workloads and their original replicas:
  kubectl get configmap curfew-state -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o yaml
